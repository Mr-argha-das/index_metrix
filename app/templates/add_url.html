Publish vacancies

Upload a CSV or XLSX sheet. Every valid row becomes a public /jobs/<number> page and is automatically sent to the Google indexing queue.
CSV or Excel (.xlsx), maximum 10 MB.

Required columns

title, company, company_url, job_details, description, qualifications, employment_type, city, region, country, date_posted, valid_through
Optional: apply_url. If omitted, job_details is used as the application/source link.

Import result

What happens after publishing?

Each valid row gets its own internal public job page. The employer's job_details URL remains a normal outbound link on that page.

The internal /jobs/<number> URL is what enters the Google indexing queue. Temporary Google failures are retried automatically.

Google accepting a notification is not proof that the page is indexed. The Dashboard separates notification, queue, crawl and index status.

New publication format: /jobs/<number>

After a source passes validation, the app generates and saves a fictional job demo with a normal link to that source. Demo details are not extracted job facts, and applications are disabled. Existing published reference pages remain unchanged.

Paste PDF or normal webpage URLs

Homepages, blogs, articles and PDFs — one URL per line, or comma separated
A .pdf extension is not required. Only http:// and https:// are accepted. Private, loopback and internal addresses are rejected by the SSRF guard.

What happens next

RECEIVED
URL recorded and queued (dedupe by normalized-URL hash).
VALIDATED
Scheme, host, DNS and private-IP checks (SSRF protection, every redirect).
CONTENT CHECKED
Detect PDF or HTML from the response. Only PDF documents require the %PDF- signature.
ANALYZED
PDF: structure, metadata and text. Webpage: title or heading, description, canonical and useful text; scripts are not executed.
PAGE PUBLISHED
A clearly labelled fictional demo at /jobs/<number>, with actual source metadata and an ordinary source link. No real vacancy is asserted.
DISCOVERY PENDING
Page added to sitemap.xml + rss.xml; search engines may discover it — not guaranteed.

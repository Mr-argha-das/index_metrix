Publish vacancy sheet

Upload a CSV/XLSX sheet. Each row becomes your own public /jobs/<number> page.
Required / supported columns
title, company, company_url, job_details, apply_url, description, qualifications, employment_type, city, region, country, date_posted, valid_through
job_details may be an employer PDF, employer job page, application form, blog/resource URL, or another public source. It is rendered as a normal followable link on your internal job page.
Flow: sheet → public /jobs page → Google notification → automatic retry on transient failures → Search Console evidence. Google notification acceptance is not the same as indexing.

Publication flow

1 · SHEET
Read each vacancy row and validate the required fields.
2 · PUBLIC PAGE
Create a server-rendered /jobs/<number> page with JobPosting data and the employer/source link.
3 · GOOGLE
Queue a Google Indexing API notification for the internal public job URL.
4 · AUTO RETRY
Transient network, quota and Google 429/5xx failures retry automatically with persistent backoff.
5 · EVIDENCE
Search Console crawl/index evidence is tracked separately from notification acceptance.

Example columns

title,company,company_url,job_details,apply_url,description,qualifications,employment_type,city,region,country,date_posted,valid_through

One vacancy per row. Duplicate vacancies are skipped and row-level errors are reported after import.

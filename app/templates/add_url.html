Add job links

Paste one or multiple employer vacancy/application URLs. Each unique URL creates one internal /jobs/<number> page.
One URL per line. Commas and spaces are also accepted. Duplicate URLs are ignored.

Automatic job creation

Every link is written to both job_details and apply_url. The remaining fields are generated using the vacancy generator logic you supplied.
The generated title/company/location/description are synthetic placeholders. Verify them against the employer source before treating a vacancy as a genuine public listing.

Import result

What happens after publishing?

Each valid row gets its own internal public job page. The employer's job_details URL remains a normal outbound link on that page.

The internal /jobs/<number> URL is what enters the Google indexing queue. Temporary Google failures are retried automatically.

Google accepting a notification is not proof that the page is indexed. The Dashboard separates notification, queue, crawl and index status.

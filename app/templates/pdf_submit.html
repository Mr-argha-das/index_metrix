Paste PDF URLs

one URL per line, or comma separated
Only http:// and https:// are accepted. Private, loopback and internal addresses are rejected by the SSRF guard.

What happens next

RECEIVED
URL recorded and queued (dedupe by normalized-URL hash).
VALIDATED
Scheme, host, DNS and private-IP checks (SSRF protection, every redirect).
PDF CHECKED
Content-Type + %PDF- signature; never trust headers alone.
ANALYZED
PyMuPDF extracts pages, metadata, SHA-256, text preview.
PAGE PUBLISHED
A useful dedicated page on our site, with a normal link to the original PDF.
DISCOVERY PENDING
Page added to sitemap.xml + rss.xml; search engines may discover it — not guaranteed.
